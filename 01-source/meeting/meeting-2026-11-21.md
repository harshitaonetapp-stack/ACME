**Date:** 21 November 2026

**Harshita:** Morning both. What's come up this week?

**Rahul:** Cancelled pickups. They're still showing on the driver's list for the day. So a customer
cancels at eight in the morning, and the driver still has it on his sheet and turns up at the address
at eleven.

**Priya:** It happened twice last week. One of them was a building with security, so he waited twenty
minutes to be let in for a collection that wasn't happening.

**Harshita:** So once a pickup is cancelled it should drop off the driver's list for that day.

**Rahul:** Yes. And out of the count as well — at the moment the day's total includes them, so it
looks like we had more work than we did.

**Priya:** That's the main one. The other thing is finding a pickup when a customer calls.

**Rahul:** They ring and say "I booked a collection for this afternoon, where is it". We've got the
phone number from the call, but there's no way to look up a pickup by phone number. You end up
scrolling the day's list looking for the address.

**Priya:** Which is fine with forty pickups. It isn't fine with two hundred.

**Harshita:** So search by the customer's phone number and get back their pickups.

**Rahul:** That would save us a lot of time on the phones.
