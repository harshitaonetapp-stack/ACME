# ACME Courier Portal — weekly call

**Date:** 24 November 2026
**Present:** Priya (ACME operations), Rahul (ACME dispatch supervisor), Harshita (OneApp)

---

**Harshita:** Thanks both. Last week's report fix is through review, so that one's done. What's come up
since?

**Priya:** The big one is rescheduling. When a customer calls and says "can you come tomorrow
instead", the team has no way to just move the pickup. They cancel it and book a new one.

**Rahul:** Which loses everything. The notes, who was assigned, when it was first booked — all gone.
And then the driver who was on it doesn't know, because as far as the system's concerned that pickup
never happened. We've had two drivers turn up at an address that was cancelled an hour earlier.

**Priya:** It also makes the day's numbers look wrong. One pickup becomes two — one cancelled, one
new — so the report shows more cancellations than we actually had.

**Harshita:** So you want to change the time on an existing pickup and keep everything else attached
to it.

**Rahul:** Yes. Same pickup, new time, same driver unless we say otherwise, and the history should
show it was moved and when.

**Priya:** It should only work for pickups that haven't happened yet, obviously. You can't reschedule
something that's already been collected.

**Harshita:** Understood. Anything else?

**Rahul:** There's something odd with driver assignment. I put Ravi on a pickup in Andheri yesterday
morning, and then without thinking I put him on another one at roughly the same time in Bandra. The
system took both. Didn't warn me, didn't stop me.

**Harshita:** It let one driver be on two pickups at once?

**Rahul:** Two at the same time, yes. We caught it because Ravi called asking which one he was meant
to go to. If he hadn't, we'd have missed a collection.

**Priya:** That's happened before actually. We assumed it was someone double-entering.

**Harshita:** Right, so the system should stop that rather than rely on the dispatcher noticing.

**Rahul:** It should at least not let it happen silently.

**Priya:** Last thing, and this isn't really for you — our finance team asked whether the contract
renewal in January is handled by your side or by their procurement. Can you check and come back to us?

**Harshita:** I'll find out and email you. Nothing technical there.

**Priya:** Perfect. That's everything from us.

**Harshita:** I'll get the rescheduling and the double-booking written up and back to you for sign-off
before anyone starts on them.
